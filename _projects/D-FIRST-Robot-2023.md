Why did we end up using COT swerve drives? 
- General lack of machining experience on the team
- Gears would need to be bought anyways
- Lack of experience slows down building cycle, want to have reliable system working early

Why switch to swerve drive? 
- Much faster in speed
- Much more mobile --- can translate in any direction, and can rotate while translating
- Opens up for more strategies like defense during matches
